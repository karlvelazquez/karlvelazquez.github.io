## Publications & Speaking

- **RSS 2025 paper** — "Cross-Modality Embedding of Force and Language for Natural Human-Robot Communication" (also titled "Demonstrating Shared Force-Language Embeddings..." on the official program page), accepted and presented in person at Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS 2025), USC, Los Angeles, June 21–25, 2025. Karl was a **main contributor** (Tejwani, Velazquez, Payne, Bonato, Asada). Affiliations span MIT, Harvard Medical School, and the Spaulding Rehabilitation Network. Archived: `publications/arxiv-preprint.md`, `publications/rss-2025-program.md`, `publications/rss-project-website.md`, `publications/github-coordinated-force-language-repo.md` (the project's code repos). Photos of him presenting: `images/rss-2025-stage-presentation-title-slide.jpeg`, `images/rss-2025-presentation-wide-stage-view.jpeg`, `images/rss-2025-welcome-screen-audience.jpeg`.
- **Nature paper** — "Adaptive robot guidance through real-time compliance estimation and dual-modal control," published in *Communications Engineering* (a Nature-portfolio journal), 5:81 (2026), DOI 10.1038/s44172-026-00632-5, published online March 19, 2026, open access (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). Karl was a **main contributor** (Tejwani, Payne, Velazquez, Bonato, Asada), working alongside John Payne on implementing the controller, designing/running the experiments, and data collection/analysis. This is a direct extension of the RSS 2025 work. Built a UR5-based adaptive physical/verbal guidance controller, validated with a Spaulding Rehabilitation observational study and a 12-person MIT user study (up to 50% tracking-error reduction, 39% smoothness improvement, 27% faster task completion). Full content archived in `publications/nature-paper.md`, including the complete abstract, contribution statement, control-theory summary, and both studies' results; code repo archived in `publications/github-robot-guidance-controller-repo.md`.
