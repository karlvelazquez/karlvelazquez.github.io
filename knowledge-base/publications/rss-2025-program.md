## Abstract Summary
The research proposes a method for creating unified embeddings that combine force profiles and language. The authors describe how "human-robot interaction could achieve this level of coordination by integrating verbal and haptic communication modalities." Their framework enables force data and words to operate within the same latent space where they can complement each other, work together, or function interchangeably. The paper addresses cross-modality embedding necessity, system architecture, data collection approaches, implementation challenges, and experimental findings.
