# DJing & Music — Hobby Detail

## How he got into it

Karl got into DJing because fraternity brothers at his MIT chapter (Theta Tau — see `research/phi-kappa-psi.md`) were already into it. He picked it up as a casual hobby that's since grown into a real passion.

## Gear

- **Software:** djay Pro, by Algoriddim
- **Controller:** Pioneer DDJ-FLX4 (this matches the "Pioneer DJ" branding visible in several of the catalogued photos — see `images/MANIFEST.md`, e.g. the Mobius Industries gala DJ set photos)

## How he approaches it

Self-describes as an "amateur," but values the hobby specifically for how accessible, portable, and flexible it is:
- Mixes casually in his own apartment.
- Will spontaneously DJ at places like Prospect Park in Brooklyn just for fun — no formal setup needed.
- The portability of a compact controller + laptop/software setup makes it easy to DJ at friends' events on short notice (consistent with the Williamsburg rooftop set and the Mobius Industries NYC gala sets already in the photo collection).
- Finds a lot of tracks via SoundCloud.

This also connects to two previously-catalogued but under-contextualized items: the MIT course CMS.303/803/21M.365 ("DJ History, Technique, and Technology") that he's documented taking (see `publications/mit-news-mixing-beats.md`), and the YouTube video "MIT Student DJ Showcase -- Deck the Dance Halls" (see `publications/youtube-video.md`) — both now read as a coherent hobby arc: picked up DJing socially via the fraternity, took the formal MIT course, appeared in a student DJ showcase, and has carried it forward as an ongoing practice (apartment sessions, spontaneous park sets, friends'-event sets, and the Mobius Industries gala).
