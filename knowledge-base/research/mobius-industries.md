## Origin story: how Karl actually ended up at Mobius (personal account, not documented anywhere online)

Karl's close friend **Matthew Pierre Louis** — founder and CEO of Möbius Industries — was a year below Karl and a fellow brother in Theta Tau at MIT (see `research/phi-kappa-psi.md`).

Karl originally planned to stay at MIT for a Master of Engineering (MEng) after finishing his undergrad degree. He lost the funding path for that plan because of federal cuts to academic research funding under the Trump administration — in Karl's own words, "there wasn't a lot of academic funding because of Trump."

Around that same time, Karl and Matthew were already planning to get an apartment together in Cambridge, independent of Karl's MEng plans. When Matthew asked what Karl's rent budget was, Karl admitted he had no funding lined up and couldn't actually afford rent. Matthew — who was building Möbius Industries at the time and needed a software engineer — offered Karl a 3-month internship so Karl could get his financial footing sorted out.

Karl ended up loving the work enough that the internship became a permanent role — "the rest is history," as he put it. He's now Head of AI, Sensing, & Software there, officially starting (per LinkedIn) in June 2025, the Monday after his Friday MIT graduation.

This resolves a previously-flagged gap in this knowledge base ("why Mobius Industries specifically") — the honest answer is a mix of a close personal friendship, a funding-driven need for an income/rent solution, and the work turning out to be something he genuinely wanted to keep doing.

## Background context (web research)

A web search for "Mobius Industries" turned up a company that appears to be a strong, confident match: **Möbius Industries**, a robotics/AI hardware startup. Notably, a search specifically combining "Mobius Industries" with "Karl Velazquez" directly surfaced Karl's own LinkedIn profile (linkedin.com/in/karl-velazquez) tied to Möbius Industries, which confirms this is the correct company rather than an unrelated namesake.

What's publicly documented about Möbius Industries:

- **What it does:** Möbius builds AI-powered robotics systems for "de-manufacturing" — an automated, full-stack process that disassembles, sorts, and refines end-of-life consumer devices and appliances (urban e-waste) to recover high-value raw materials like steel, aluminum, copper, cobalt, and lithium, aiming for over 95% material purity. The company frames this as "urban mining": an alternative to traditional overseas mining that uses less energy and produces fewer emissions, reducing U.S. dependence on imported materials. The technology stack combines robotics, AI/sensing, and materials science.
- **Founding:** Founded in 2024 by Matthew Pierre-Louis, an MIT graduate with hardware/mechanical engineering experience from Tesla, per Recycling Today and the company's EWOR startup profile.
- **Location:** Headquartered in the Boston/Cambridge, Massachusetts area, with plans (per the company's own careers page) to relocate its office to New York City around May 2026 — consistent with Karl's description of being NYC-based.
- **Funding:** Completed a $3.8 million pre-seed funding round (announced around October 2025), led by Outlander VC, with participation from Mana Ventures, GoAhead, Entropy Industrial, EWOR, E14, Plug and Play, Thursday Ventures, and a group of strategic angel investors. Legal work on the round was handled by Crowell & Moring, per that firm's own press release.
- **Culture note:** per the company's careers page, Möbius has stated that at this early stage all employees — "from the CEO to a mechanical engineer" — are paid the same, non-negotiable salary.
